This Privacy Policy explains how KARJENG PIDAI STUDIO handles information when you use our AI-powered film and drama production application.

Information is used to operate the application, process AI requests, manage creative projects, retrieve authorized files, deliver generated media, and maintain service security.

If you connect Google Drive, the application accesses files according to the permissions you authorize. You may revoke access through your Google Account settings.

Project resources may be stored in connected storage services. Generated media may be temporarily stored on processing servers and deleted after successful delivery or when no longer needed.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application or disclosed when legally required.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You may disconnect integrations, stop using the service, and request deletion of applicable information where feasible.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, please contact the developer through the KARJENG PIDAI STUDIO GitHub repository.
